# Shutter Thoughts

Welcome to Shutter Thoughts, a personal blog where I share my journey. I
believe that photography is not about having the best camera or equipment. It is
about what the picture means to you and capturing moments that speak to you.

I initially started with Instagram to share my photos, but over time I grew tired the AI edited images, the bots, the algorithm. No judgment towards anyone, it just wasn't for me.

I miss the days when people built their own little corners of the internet, personal websites and blogs made out of passion, when the web felt more like an ocean with small islands here and there rather than a strip mall around the corner. 

I'm a programmer by trade based in Berkely, CA. I did my schooling in Davis CA (Go Ags!), and starting this blog is my way of reviving that old school spirit, creating something personal and genuine.

On this blog you'll find my growing collection of photographs as I explore more places and blog posts
about my experiences, and maybe some rants about the changing world.
