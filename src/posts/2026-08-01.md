summary: Day 7 of the SoCal Road Trip - On the way to San Francisco stopped at La Purisima Mission. The mission apparently seved as a stronghold during the 1824 Chumash Uprising. It operated as a massive, self-contained agricultural and industrial and developed vast systems to feed, clothe, and support its population. It has 2 sacturies built side by side The Main Mission Church (La Iglesia) and The Padre’s Chapel (La Capilla de los Padres).
